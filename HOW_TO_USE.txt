HOW TO USE: projected-singing-speech
====================================

PURPOSE
  Model the acoustic, aerodynamic, and resonant transformations between conversational speech,
  projected public oratory, and singing/chanting (vibrato, singer's formant cluster, subglottal pressure).

CAPABILITIES
  - Model 3 operational modes: Conversational (65 dBA) vs Projected Oratory (82 dBA) vs Singing (88+ dBA)
  - Quantify subglottal pressure scaling (Psub: 6.5 -> 18.0 -> 24.0+ cm H2O) and closed quotient rise
  - Measure Speaker's/Singer's Formant Cluster boost (+6 to +12 dB in 2.8-3.5 kHz range)
  - Detail pitch vibrato mechanics: rate (Hz), excursion depth (semitones), and onset latency (ms)
  - Model vowel modification (passaggio covering) adjustments at high pitch thresholds
  - Output machine-readable projection_singing_profile.json

USE CASES
  - "How does this politician's voice change when shouting in an arena vs talking in a podcast?"
  - You want to synthesize a singing voice or rhythmic chant from a speaking voice profile
  - You need to model the epilaryngeal resonance peak that lets a voice cut through background noise
  - Calculating subglottal pressure and aerodynamic loading for vocal health simulations

IDEAS
  - Pair with affect-register-atlas when modeling high-energy rally speeches
  - Inject the 2.8-3.5 kHz formant boost into KlattGrid in synthesis-control-compiler
  - Enable vibrato LFO only during sustained singing notes
